# Growth Funnel Opportunity Map — ImagineArt

> One opportunity identified per funnel stage, using real public signals: coded Trustpilot reviews, competitor comparison, and public app-store/review data. Where a stage needed more than the reviews gave me, I note it as a flagged gap rather than guessing.

## Acquisition
**Real signal:** two of 49 coded reviews describe landing on ImagineArt via a search ad for a *different* named product (Google Flow, Leonardo AI), realizing only after paying.
**Opportunity:** audit paid search ad copy/keyword targeting for brand confusion with competitor names — this risks acquiring users who churn immediately once they realize the mismatch, which is wasted spend, not just a support problem.
**What I'd need to validate further:** real ad spend and keyword data (not public) — flagging this as a hypothesis worth testing with the paid/marketing team, not a confirmed finding.

## Activation
**Real signal:** one review (Onboarding/UX category) describes a "pay to learn" experience — credits were consumed figuring out the tool before producing a usable result.
**Opportunity:** test a short, free, guided "first creation" flow (template-based, not blank canvas) to reduce credit burn before a user's first success — directly maps to the JD's "help users reach their first successful creation."
**Metric to test:** % of new users who complete a first successful generation within their first session.

## Engagement / Retention
**Real signal:** Billing/Refund complaints (42% of negative reviews) — covered in full in `PRD.md`. Separately, Output Quality complaints (5 of 33) mention inconsistency across a multi-image project, which risks disengaging users mid-project.
**Opportunity (primary, already scoped):** checkout/cancellation clarity (see main PRD).
**Opportunity (secondary, flagged for later):** character/style consistency across a session — worth a follow-up review-mining pass focused only on this category if time allows.

## Referral / Growth Loops
**Real signal:** none found in the review data — reviews don't mention sharing, inviting, or referral mechanics at all, positive or negative.
**Honest read:** this is a gap in the current product experience, not just in my data. A reasonable hypothesis: creations aren't easily shareable in a way that pulls in new users (no visible "made with ImagineArt" loop in what reviewers describe).
**What I'd test:** a lightweight share/attribution prompt after a successful creation, with a referral incentive, and measure new-user signups attributed to shared content.

## Summary table

| Stage | Opportunity | Evidence strength |
|---|---|---|
| Acquisition | Brand-confusion risk in paid search | Weak (2 reviews) — flagged as hypothesis |
| Activation | Credit burn before first success | Weak (1 review) — flagged as hypothesis |
| Engagement/Retention | Checkout & cancellation clarity | **Strong** (14 of 33 negative reviews) — fully scoped in PRD |
| Referral | No sharing loop visible | Absence of evidence, treated as a gap, not a finding |

This table itself is a useful artifact: it shows you can tell the difference between a well-evidenced opportunity and a hunch worth testing — which is exactly what a growth role needs you to do with messier, real data.
